Radar

Activity on a scope, not a target

One receiver paints a ring. The ring is a coarse near, mid, or far ranking of disturbance strength, and it has no angle. Three or more receivers can draw one blob: a weighted centroid of the positions you place, with an uncertainty radius. Blips mean movement-like activity. They are not people, and they are not a count.
