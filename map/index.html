Rooms

Draw the home

The plan is a drawing on this machine, not a measurement in meters. Wi-Fi shows which room was active. It does not estimate a position inside the room.

Place two sensors on the drawing to see an estimated sensing line. It is a geometric sketch, not a measured Fresnel zone and not a distance in meters.

Drag a room, or draw one. A door and a zone are labels on the drawing. Alerts use the room name.

Walk test

Walk the rooms. This computer records which room names show movement-like activity, then suggests those rooms as zones you can accept or edit. A phone is not part of this test. The Radar page can turn this drawing with the phone compass. That turn is a display heading. It is not this walk test, not a measured wall, and not a location.
