Hardware to buy before trusting any of this

  • Eight ESP32-S3 dev boards to start, two on each side of a 4 m square. Sixteen is the denser follow-up.
  • A USB cable per board and a powered hub for flashing. Write down whether each antenna is the PCB trace or an external one.
  • Floor tape and a tape measure for a 0.5 m grid. A second person clicks session annotations when a foot lands on a mark.
  • Two boards kept for a separate FTM session. Do not mix FTM and CSI on one radio until that has been tested. No antenna-array NIC for this first pass.

Placement, the recording order, and the JSON the evaluation script accepts are in docs/research/experiment-plan.md. The script refuses simulation.
